SHOTSTOPPER

One goalkeeper, played with one finger in front of your camera. They shoot until you've let three in, and it only gets faster.

↔Point your index finger up and move it. The keeper follows.

↑Lift it to jump. Keep it up for a full leap — the only way to the top of the goal.

↓Bend it down for the splits. Wide and low, but no height.

⇆Flick it sideways to dive at a corner you can't reach on your feet.

Save it on your feet: 1 point.
Save it with a leap, a dive or the splits: 2 points. Ten saves in a row wins back a ball you've let in.

Uses your camera. The video stays on your device.

FINGER CONTROL

Starting your camera…

↔Point your index finger up and move it left and right. The keeper follows your fingertip.

↑Lift your finger to jump. Keep it up there for a full leap.

↓Bend your finger down for the splits.

⇆Flick it sideways, fast, to dive that way.
